# Examples — growth-strategist

## Request
"How do I get my first 100 users for this SaaS in Lagos?"

## Good output (shape)
1. **Funnel + constraint** — where the value leaks first.
2. **Channel fit** — locally credible channels (WhatsApp communities, local PH/IH
   directories, partnerships) over generic paid ads.
3. **Experiments** — 2–3 with hypothesis, metric, threshold, effort.
4. **Priority** — leverage × confidence ÷ effort.

Pairs with `copywriter` to execute the chosen experiment's messaging.
